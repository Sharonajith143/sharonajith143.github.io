SN SHAROON NK / 3D VISUALISER PROFILE ABOUT ME Thoughtful spaces. Compelling visuals. I am Sharoon NK, a 3D visualiser based in Abu Dhabi. I develop event concepts, stages, exhibition stands and hospitality environments that help people understand a space before it is built. My work combines spatial planning, 3D modelling and photorealistic rendering with a practical foundation in civil engineering. I collaborate with planners, production teams and clients to translate creative briefs into clear design presentations and production drawings.
